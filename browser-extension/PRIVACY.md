# Privacy policy - MeshDepot Importer

## Who receives your data

Nobody but you. This extension has no server of its own. Everything it sends
goes to the MeshDepot instance whose address you enter in its options - a server
you run yourself. The developer receives nothing. There is no telemetry, no
analytics and no third-party service involved.

## What is sent to your MeshDepot instance

When you press the import button on a model page: the address of that page, the
download links for its files, and the design's title, description, pictures and
tags. Nothing is sent unless you press import.

Download links from these platforms can carry a time-limited access token, so
that your own server is able to fetch the files. Such a link is passed on
unchanged and is only valid for that one download.

## What is stored on your device

The address of your MeshDepot instance, its API key, and your two settings -
whether the browser download is cancelled once the link has been handed over,
and whether imports are added to a collection. These stay in the browser's local
extension storage. The API key is sent to your own instance, because that is
what authenticates you there, and nowhere else.

## Cookies

On MakerWorld the extension reads the session cookie of the site in order to
make requests to MakerWorld in your name - the same session you are already
signed in with. It is used for those requests only. Cookies of other sites are
not read, and no cookie is sent to any party other than the site it belongs to.

## What is not collected

No browsing history, no pages other than the model page you import from, no
personal profile data, no passwords and no two-factor codes. The extension uses
the browser session you already have precisely so that no platform credentials
ever have to be handed over.

## Host permission

The optional permission for any address exists because you choose where your
MeshDepot server runs, so its address cannot be known in advance. It is only
ever used for the address you enter yourself, and is not requested until you
save one.
